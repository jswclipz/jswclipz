Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

jswclipz is a personal desktop application used only by its owner to make short video clips and send them to the owner's own TikTok account.

What data jswclipz accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, jswclipz may access, with the owner's permission:

  • basic profile information (display name and avatar), to show which account is connected;
  • the list of the owner's public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts, to show which clips perform well;
  • permission to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, which the owner then reviews and posts in the TikTok app.

How data is stored and used

All data stays on the owner's own computer. jswclipz has no servers and does not sell, share or transfer TikTok data to anyone. Access tokens are stored locally and are used only to perform the actions above.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect jswclipz at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, and can delete the app's local data by deleting its folder.
